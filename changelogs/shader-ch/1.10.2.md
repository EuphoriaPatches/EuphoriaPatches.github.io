Please look at the 1.10.0 changes, this is just a small bugfix update.

### Changes

- Updated to Complementary Shaders r5.9.2 [YOUTUBE SHOWCASE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xu8--_-_qhQ)
- New WindLink features added: floating particles on water, waving snow layers on leaves, hanging sign support, foliage interaction with the player. All thanks to [@PlunderPixels](https://github.com/plunderpixels)
- Make the entity damage overlay not be affected by darkness desaturation
- Initial Distant Horizons texture support, quite good for now. Needs a DH version that has that feature. I have no clue if it's released yet or not, I just did what IMS told me to add.
- Darkness desaturation for spider eyes follows the alpha now for intensity when the player is not near a light source (for better mod compatibility)
- Added more modded blocks to the [properties files](https://github.com/EuphoriaPatches/propertiesFiles) thanks to the contributors <3
- Updated [translations](https://www.euphoriapatches.com/lang) thanks to the contributors <3


### Fixes

- Fix sculk veins not being reflected in world space reflections
